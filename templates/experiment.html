Experiment Methodology

To evaluate the accuracy of our Proposed Dynamic ETA system, we generated a simulated test suite consisting of 550 historical bus trips. We evaluated two separate paradigms:

Baseline Schedule-Only System:

Uses static planned stops schedules only. Completely ignores current traffic delays, passenger attendance boarding times, and real-time vehicle GPS positions.

Proposed Dynamic System:

Calculates dynamic ETA = Current Time + Remaining Travel Time (derived from live GPS coordinates) + Boarding Dwell Time (calculated from student presence) + Traffic Delays.
